Privacy Policy

MXNDR analytics is a private tool used by one person: the independent musician MXNDR (Max Ender), the owner of this site. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

What it connects to

The tool connects only to social accounts owned by the operator, using the official APIs of those platforms after the operator explicitly authorises the connection. It never connects to anyone else's account.

What data it accesses

Only the operator's own account data: the operator's own posts and videos, their public performance figures (views, likes, comments, shares, saves), the captions the operator wrote, and comments left on the operator's own posts. It does not access private messages, follower lists, or any data belonging to other accounts beyond what is publicly attached to a comment on the operator's own post.

Where the data goes

The data is read on demand and processed on the operator's own computer. It is not uploaded to any third-party service, not sold, not shared, not used for advertising, and not used to build profiles of any person. No database of other people's data is maintained.

What it writes

Where the platform allows it, the tool may publish posts and reply to comments on the operator's own account, and only when the operator explicitly instructs it to do so.

Retention and deletion

Cached figures are kept on the operator's own machine for as long as they are useful for analysis and can be deleted at any time. Revoking the app's access in the platform's own settings immediately ends all access.
